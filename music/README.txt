MY MUSIC (car radio station)
============================
Copy your own audio files (.mp3, .m4a, .ogg, .wav, .aac, .flac) into this folder.
In the game, get into any car, bike, auto or bus and press R until the radio shows "My Music".
Songs play in random order. New files are picked up the next time you start the game.

Only use music you own or have the right to play.

OPTIONAL: internet radio
------------------------
To add internet radio stations, create a file called streams.json in this folder, e.g.

[
  { "name": "My Favourite Station", "url": "https://example.com/stream.mp3" }
]

Each entry becomes a station on the car radio. Use the official stream links published by the station.
